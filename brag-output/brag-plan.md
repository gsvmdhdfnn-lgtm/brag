# Brag plan: Josh Evans Soccer School (joshevans.co.uk)

## Rubric
- **What:** A Surrey football coaching school that has coached kids aged 4–15 since 2010. It runs weekly sessions, an Elite Academy, school PE and holiday camps.
- **Who:** Parents of young players in Surrey & South West London.
- **For them:** "A safe and secure environment to gain confidence, improve their skills, realise their potential and most of all, have fun playing the game."
- **What sets it apart:** It coaches 2,500+ children a week. The founder became Chelsea's youngest professional coach at 15. A clear Player Path runs from Little Legends to the Elite Academy, and the Academy has played Crystal Palace, AFC Wimbledon, Reading and Bristol City.
- **Most impressive claim:** "Now coach over 2500 children a week."
- **Visual hook:** The school's own cinematic film ("BE-ON-IT.mp4"): golden-hour pitches, blue kit, ball at boot level.
- **Real UI to show:** The live homepage banner "2 WEEKS OF FOOTBALL CAMPS THIS OCTOBER 2026 HALF-TERM – BOOK NOW!" with its "Book now!" button.
- **Tone:** `cinematic` mixed with sports-brand energy. It plays it straight with big type, hard cuts on the beat and warm footage.
- **Share caption:** see share-copy.txt

## Angle
The home page sells it as grassroots football, but the numbers say it's a talent pipeline. The video leads with the scale, shows the pathway, and ends on the timely ask: October half-term camps, booking now. It closes on the school's own line, "Be on it. Believe in it. Be you."

## Visual identity
- Brand blue `#226ab4` (site nav and banner), deep navy `#0e2f57` for dark plates, white, and the logo's greens and cyans as accents (`#00d084`, `#66cef8`)
- Signika for headings and Open Sans for body text, both loaded by the site
- Real logo (`logo1.jpg`), real hero film, real homepage screenshot

## Music
Bundled `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9` (114.8 BPM). Its bar grid is 1.07, 3.18, 5.28, 7.40, 9.50 …, with a beat every ≈0.522s. Cuts land on beats. The track fades out over the last 1.2s.
